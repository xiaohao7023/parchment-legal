Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Where are my files stored?

A: All your Markdown files are stored locally on your device. We never upload or access your files.

Q: Can I import files from other apps?

A: Yes! Use the "Open File" button to import .md files from Files, WeChat, or other apps that support sharing.

Q: How do I save a Markdown file as an image?

A: Open a document, tap the "..." menu, and select "Save as Image". The image will be saved to your Photos.

Q: Does the app work offline?

A: Yes, Markdown Viewer works completely offline. No internet connection required.

Q: Can I edit Markdown files?

A: Yes! Tap the "..." menu while viewing a document and select "Edit" to enter editing mode.

Q: Why does the app need photo library access?

A: Photo library access is only used when you save a Markdown file as an image. We never access your existing photos.
